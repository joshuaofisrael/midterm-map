## Still limited

- Puerto Rico is outside this list and its candidate file is still unpublished.
- The District of Columbia uses the Board of Elections candidate list. Ballotpedia’s 2026 elections page did not include a parseable candidate table, so no extra offices were added from it.
- Louisiana’s November 3 U.S. House election is an open primary. Ballotpedia marks those rows Primary, not General. The six districts are stored from that primary list. Profile links for those rows are unverified when the general-election matcher does not accept a Primary status.
- West Virginia’s parsed general-election list names U.S. Senate and two U.S. House districts and no governor, attorney general, secretary of state, or supreme court contest.
- Kentucky, Maryland, and Oklahoma supreme court contests are limited to the seats present on the parsed general-election list.
- State legislative districts, public-service-commission districts, and intermediate appellate courts are omitted. This site has no place-to-legislative-district assignment for them.
- A candidate with no verified Ballotpedia profile is shown without a link and listed in docs/ballotpedia-unresolved.md.
